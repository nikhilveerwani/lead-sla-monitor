Every lead called within 24 hours

Speed to first call is the cheapest conversion lever a sales team has. This monitor checks every lead against a 24-hour first-call SLA, shows which teams and reps are slipping, lists the leads to call right now, and posts a digest to Slack three times a day.

How it's built

  1. The SLA is the first call attempt within 24 clock hours of the lead being created. Attempts count, not only connects: the SLA measures speed, and connect rate is a separate metric.
  2. Calls logged before the lead was created are ignored. They belong to an earlier registration of the same person and would make a missed lead look on time.
  3. Leads still inside their window are "Running" and stay out of SLA %, so yesterday's number isn't dragged down by leads that still have time.
  4. Periods follow the lead's creation date in IST: yesterday, Monday to yesterday, and the 1st to yesterday.
  5. Reasons survive the refresh. The breached tab is rebuilt every run, but the team's Reason and Comment entries are stored by lead ID and written back.
