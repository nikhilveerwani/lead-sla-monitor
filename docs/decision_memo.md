# Decision memo: closing the lead-response gap

**To:** Head of Sales, sales managers · **From:** Analytics · **Data:** synthetic demo, last 45 days

> Figures are from the live demo on the day this memo was written. The [live executive summary](https://nikhilveerwani.github.io/lead-sla-monitor/) recomputes them three times a day.

## The decision

About 9% of leads miss the 24-hour first-call SLA. Is that a rep-performance problem to coach, or a process problem to fix?

## What we found

1. **The biggest gap is the calendar, not the people.** Saturday leads are called on time 55% of the time against 95% Monday to Thursday: a 40-point gap (95% CI 36–45, p < 0.001). Nobody calls on Sunday, so a lead created Saturday afternoon is past 24 hours by Monday morning.
2. **Only one rep is significantly below the team average** (Zoya). The other 11 reps differ by amounts that are within normal variation for about 200 leads a month each. Coaching them on this metric would be reacting to noise.
3. **Late calls outnumber missed ones.** Of this month's breaches, about two-thirds were called late and one-third never called. The two need different fixes: scheduling for the late ones, a worked overdue list for the rest.
4. **A large share of breaches carry a process reason** in the team's own notes: leads not synced to the CRM, reassigned late, calls from personal phones that never got logged. Several of these are system issues that look like rep misses.

## Options

| Option | Upside | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| A. Push all reps harder on the SLA | Simple message | Blames 11 reps for a calendar problem; doesn't touch Saturday |
| **B. Fix the weekend, coach the one outlier (recommended)** | Targets the biggest, proven gap | Needs weekend staffing |
| C. Change the SLA to "24 working hours" | Number improves overnight | The lead's experience doesn't; it hides the problem |

## Recommendation: Option B

1. **Add a 4-hour Sunday calling block**, or a Saturday 6–9 pm block if Sunday isn't possible. At the weekday on-time rate, that rescues about **200 leads a month**.
2. **Coach Zoya individually**, using the breach reasons to separate rep habits from system issues first.
3. **Fix the two system causes** that show up as misses: CRM sync failures and calls from unlogged personal phones.
4. **Keep the SLA at 24 clock hours.** It measures what the lead experiences.

## What would change this recommendation

- A Sunday pilot shows Saturday leads called on Sunday convert no better than those called on Monday: then the gap is cosmetic, and the money is better spent elsewhere.
- Breach reasons show most of Zoya's misses are reassigned or unsynced leads: then it's a routing fix, not coaching.
